## Research questions by area

### Inventory browsing
- At what stock size do references introduce filters? Which filters come first?
- Default sort and how it is labelled.
- Grid vs list on mobile; cards per viewport.
- How empty results and unavailable inventory are communicated.

### Vehicle cards
- Which 3–5 facts appear on cards; order; how price is emphasized.
- How Reserved/Sold is marked on cards (if shown at all).
- Image aspect ratio and count indicators.

### VDP
- Section order on mobile; what is above the fold.
- Gallery behavior (swipe, full-screen, counter, video).
- CTA set, labels and placement; sticky behavior.
- How specs are grouped; how missing data is handled.
- How Sold VDPs behave (removed, redirected, marked).

### Mobile conversion
- WhatsApp vs call vs form prominence in UAE references.
- Prefilled message content.
- Number of steps to request a test drive or viewing.

### Trust
- Dealer identity: address, map, hours, licensing mentions.
- Use of real photos vs studio/stock.
- Sold cars or delivered-customer proof: format and placement.
- Which trust claims depend on services we do not offer (inspection, warranty) — avoid copying.

## Hypotheses (unverified)

- H1: UAE buyers expect WhatsApp as a primary contact method on VDPs.
- H2: A sticky mobile CTA bar on the VDP increases lead actions versus in-page CTAs only.
- H3: For a small stock, a simple list without filters performs as well as a filtered grid.
- H4: Single-dealer references show sold cars in a separate archive rather than mixed into stock.
- H5: Regional spec (e.g. GCC) is a key card fact for UAE used cars.

Each hypothesis needs observation records before it drives a material design decision.
