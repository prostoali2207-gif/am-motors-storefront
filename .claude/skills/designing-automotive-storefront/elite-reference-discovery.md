# Elite reference discovery for AM Motors storefront

Canonical method: https://github.com/prostoali2207-gif/professional-ai-agents/blob/main/architect/methodology/excellence-reference-discovery.md
Read current upstream if accessible; local minimum when offline:

1. Define precise task: e.g. mobile vehicle listing, VDP fact order, true photo gallery, clear WhatsApp entry for a small-stock Ajman showroom. Distinguish craft, usability and evidence of lead quality.
2. Discover independently: live UAE original dealers and marketplaces, small-stock and specialty dealers, expert web/UX studies and curated work, maker/team portfolios and credible outside-category commerce patterns. Do not only recycle a frozen big-marketplace list.
3. Inspect actual original live mobile page and customer path. Record page, viewport, date, important information, states, CTA and task behavior. If blocked label NOT INSPECTED, never claim performance from screenshot, award or SEO rank.
4. Choose a small complementary group: DIRECT (same small-showroom job) and ADJACENT (transferable mechanism). Record original URL, provenance, observed behavior, relevance, limitations and ADOPT/ADAPT/REJECT/TEST outcome.
5. Protect AM Motors approved real vehicle data, assets, prices and claims; never copy competitor imagery or imply unverified guarantees, reviews or finance. Verify our implementation with real mobile task checks.
6. Stop when another source no longer changes design decisions. Routine fixes do not require full research; no formal qualification.
